## 5. What this does and does not claim

- It **does** claim the delivered Chandrayaan-2 geolocation is off by kilometres
  on the strips that locked, in a consistent direction, and that our own chain
  is verified to within one browse pixel against the same reference.
- It does **not** claim a value for strips that did not lock. Those are reported
  as failures, not omitted.
- The offset is modelled as a pure translation. Whether it varies along a
  101 000-line strip is untested and is the obvious next experiment.
- Every product label carries `corners_refined = False`; ISRO does not claim
  these are photogrammetrically refined. This measures how large that is.
